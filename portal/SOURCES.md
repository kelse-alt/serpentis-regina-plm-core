## Design direction

- Founder-supplied master blueprint: https://app.notion.com/p/SERPENTIS-REGINA-COM-255bab06d9b4802fb8dcfc4d396f6446
- Existing customer-facing atelier and head-logo reference: https://www.serpentisregina.com/
- Founder-supplied 3D folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HLX5S5KM13apPqvJSkcgrytp5rNggKm1

The accessible blueprint calls for three sculpted heads, marble plinths, warm blush surroundings, soft spotlights, 360-degree interaction and a slight enlargement/swirl/fade on entry. The founder selected a reflective, colorful finish and specifically pointed to the head in the live site's top-left corner. Its pink-contoured pearl appearance and the supplied colorful rendering inform this material interpretation.
